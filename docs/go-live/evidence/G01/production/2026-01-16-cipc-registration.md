## Not established by this evidence

- Company Secretary appointment. The sponsor states it is yet to be formalised, so Brenda Adams does not yet hold the office in a verifiable way. Confirm it against CIPC records, and that any appointment has been filed, before her sign-off is relied on. Director status is as at the certificate date (16/01/2026); later changes are not covered.
- Shareholders.
- Beneficial ownership. The certificate itself says beneficial ownership must be filed with CIPC within 10 business days of registration; filing status is unverified.
- Annual return, VAT, PAYE, POPIA and Information Officer status.
- Subsidiary incorporation, ownership percentages and platform IP assignment (D-02, D-03).
- Control of `nabhold.com` and `www.nabhold.com` (named by the sponsor as the estate domains; DNS ownership is unverified).

## Independence note

Brenda Adams was named as an intended onboarding requester and Company Secretary; neither that name nor the unformalised appointment is an authorised CP Principal. Brian James Nabusiu is recorded as a director on the January certificate; current director and representative authority require their own evidence. Registration review need not wait for a Company Secretary appointment: use an independently authorised reviewer who did not assert the claim, and an independent authorised decider, with the required CP scopes and immutable decision evidence. Do not use a director’s own assertion as independent verification. The Control Plane maker/checker requirements remain in force.
